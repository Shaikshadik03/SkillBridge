SIH26044 • Ministry of Ayush / MSDE

SkillBridge 🎓

Know Your Fit, Close Your Gap

An Intelligent Portal for Academia-Industry Skill Mapping, Dynamic Match Scoring, and Automated Learning Roadmaps.

Team: Antigravity Innovators • Track: Career-Tech / Skill Mapping
The Challenge

Blind Applications & High Rejection Rates

  • 🔴
    Application Fatigue: College students apply to 50+ internships blindly without knowing if they meet real prerequisites.
  • 🔴
    Zero Feedback: Standard portals reject candidates without explaining what specific skills they were missing.
  • 🔴
    Curriculum Mismatch: Academic coursework lags behind fast-moving industry requirements by 2–3 years.
Existing Gap

Static Portals Offer No Actionable Insights

  • ⚠️
    Wall of Text Requirements: Internshala, LinkedIn, and Naukri list 15 skills per post with zero personalized fit scores.
  • ⚠️
    No Learning Direction: Students don't know which high-ROI skill will unlock the most opportunities.
  • ⚠️
    Placement Cell Blindness: Colleges cannot see aggregate skill deficiencies across their batch to organize workshops.
Proposed Solution

Dynamic Match Engine & Gap Analyzer

  • ✅
    Real-Time Compatibility Math: Computes overlap percentage between student skills and required competencies on the fly.
  • ✅
    Transparent Visual Breakdown: Clearly marks possessed skills in Green and missing prerequisites in Red.
  • ✅
    Aggregated Gap Prioritization: Groups missing skills across top dream roles into a prioritized learning list with Chart.js.
Technical Details

Reactive Client Engine & Scalable Analytics

  • 💻
    Frontend: Modern HTML5, Responsive CSS3, and Reactive ES6 State.
  • ⚙️
    Matching Engine: Set intersection & overlap calculation executing in <1ms.
  • 📊
    Visualizations: Chart.js frequency histograms for skill gap distribution.
  • 📁
    Data Tier: Standardized JSON competency taxonomies and LocalStorage state.
Future Scope

Bridging India's Graduate Employability Gap

  • 🚀
    Impact: Turns job hunting from discouraging rejection into an empowering, gamified skill progression journey.
  • 🚀
    Curated Learning Links: Auto-recommending free NPTEL, YouTube, and Coursera tutorials for each missing skill.
  • 🚀
    Placement Cell Dashboard: Institutional analytics helping colleges identify student skill trends and partner with hiring companies.
